Become a Garden Partner

Garden Partners come on a set schedule, monthly or quarterly, and make an annual gift in an amount they choose. In return your team gets standing dates booked a year ahead, a tree in the grove, and a named garden area at the larger levels. We are choosing our first four founding partners now.

Quick answers

Is there a minimum group size?

No. Tell us your size and we'll find the right project.

Do we need gardening experience?

No. We'll show you everything. Willingness to get your hands dirty matters more.

What do volunteers work on?

Planting, harvesting, weeding, mulching, trimming, and building or repairing garden beds. Projects change with the season.

What if it rains or it's too hot?

We'll contact your group organizer and move you to the next open date.

Where do we park and meet?

We send parking and meeting details to your organizer before the day.
